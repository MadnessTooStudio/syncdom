Classification & Risk Assessment Methodology

The United Nations Global Sovereign Domain Registry catalogues official state portals and national network gateways across recognized and non-standard top-level domains. Domains designated as Restricted are operated by jurisdictions flagged by the International Telecommunications Security Committee due to elevated geopolitical risk, unverified cryptographic governance, or regional instability.
